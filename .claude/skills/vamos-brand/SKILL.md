# VAMOS brand

VAMOS sells classic and current football jerseys plus its own cotton tees, from Kuwait, shipping to 29 countries.
Tagline: **Home of Classic Jerseys**. Site: `www.vamos-kw.com`.

The brand is **black + neon lime, high contrast, sharp geometry, dark and premium**. If a design looks light, soft, pastel,
paper-textured or "generic template", it is off-brand. That mistake has happened before: a light grey paper background
with small corner streaks was rejected as "outside the VAMOS style".

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Lime | `#9CD00D` | the brand colour (sampled from a VAMOS asset). Big flat fields, the V, highlights |
| Neon lime (glow) | `#A8F01E` | light only: rim light, haze, glow on dark scenes |
| Lime shade | `#7DB51F` | the darker face of a lime surface (box side), pressed states |
| Black | `#0D0D0D` | the other half of every layout; backgrounds, the V, type on lime |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | type on black, sparingly |

- Two colours carry everything: lime and black. White is for reading, not for decoration.
- No grey paper, no beige, no gradients into other hues, no extra accent colours.
- Dark scenes: deep black with a soft lime haze/fog and lime rim light (the bag photo look).
- Flat scenes: full lime field with a black V (the box look), or full black with a lime V.

## Signature graphics

1. **The giant V chevron.** The box is split by one huge V running edge to edge: lime outside, black inside (or the reverse).
   It is the main graphic device: use it as the layout split, the scene transition (a V that closes and opens), and the frame for product shots.
2. **Double diagonal bars.** Two thick parallel black slashes cut into a corner (top left on the bag). Use them as a secondary accent and a wipe.
3. **The mark `\V/`.** A V flanked by two short slashes, often shown above the wordmark. Use the real file; never redraw it.
4. **Wordmark `VAMOS®`.** Geometric sans, the A drawn as a Λ (no crossbar), registered mark top right. Never retype it in a font; use the real logo file.

## Packaging and real products

- **Box:** tall rectangle, lime with the giant black V on the front, a rounded window cut-out where the shirt shows through,
  `VAMOS®` across the top, the `\V/ VAMOS` lockup vertical on the side, customer service number + QR + `vamos-kw.com` on the bottom.
- **Bag:** neon lime paper bag, black rope handles, the two black diagonal bars top left, `\V/` + `VAMOS®` centered, small `\V/` bottom left.
- **Tees:** cotton crew-neck in black, charcoal heather, grey heather and white; small `VAMOS` print centered on the chest,
  printed neck label "VAMOS / Home of Classic Jerseys / 100% Cotton".
- Show the real products whenever possible. In AI generation, start image-to-video from the real product photo so logos survive;
  never ask a model to invent a VAMOS logo or packaging.

## Type

- Latin: the logo only, plus short uppercase labels in a geometric sans (Jost or similar), tracked out.
- Arabic: heavy, bold, sporty (Rubik 800-900, Tajawal 800, Cairo 800). Big and short.
- Arabic rules from `motion-design` apply: no letter-spacing on Arabic, reveal per word, wipes right to left.
- Ad copy for the Gulf audience: simple Modern Standard Arabic unless asked for Kuwaiti dialect. Voiceover text fully diacritised.

## Tone

Confident, football, premium, short. One idea per frame. No fake numbers, no invented claims
(shipping speed, durability, "best quality") unless the owner confirmed them.

## Motion

- Energy of a sports broadcast: hard cuts and V wipes on the beat, fast entrances, faster exits.
- Lime haze drifts, the V moves, nothing sits dead still.
- Music: confident and energetic (afro house / football anthem / cinematic sports). Avoid ambient pads that feel strange or sad.
  A synth pad bed with an E-minor kick was rejected as "a weird feeling".

## AI prompt suffix (add to every VAMOS image or video prompt)

```
Dark moody environment, deep black background, neon lime green (#9CD00D) rim light and soft lime haze, high contrast, premium sportswear commercial look. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no random graphics.
```
(Drop the "no logos" part only when the first frame is a real VAMOS product photo.)

## Checklist before delivering anything VAMOS

- [ ] Only lime + black (+ white type). No grey paper or light, soft backgrounds.
- [ ] The V chevron or the diagonal bars appear as a real design element.
- [ ] Real logo file, not a font imitation.
- [ ] Real products (tees, box, bag) shown where the story needs them.
- [ ] Arabic big, bold, per-word reveals; no letter-spacing.
- [ ] No invented claims or numbers.
